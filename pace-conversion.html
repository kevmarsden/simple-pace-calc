Pace Conversion: Min/Km to Min/Mile

Convert your running pace between minutes per kilometer and minutes per mile, and see the matching speed in mph and km/h.

How to convert pace

One mile is 1.609 kilometers. To convert a pace per kilometer to a pace per mile, multiply it by 1.609. To go from pace per mile to pace per kilometer, divide by 1.609.

Pace per mile = Pace per km × 1.609

For example, a 5:00 per kilometer pace is 8:03 per mile (5 minutes × 1.609 = 8.05 minutes, or 8:03). To turn a pace into speed, divide 60 by the pace in minutes: 8:00 per mile is 60 ÷ 8 = 7.5 mph.
