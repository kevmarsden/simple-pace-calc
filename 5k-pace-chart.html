5K Pace Chart

The 5K is the most popular race distance in the world and a great benchmark for your fitness, whether it is your first race or you are chasing a new personal best.

A 5K is 3.1 miles (5 kilometers). Find your goal finish time below to see the pace per mile and per kilometer you need to hold, plus the 400 m lap time for track workouts.

For example, to run a 5K in 20:00, you need to average 6:26 per mile (4:00 per kilometer). Need a time that is not listed? Use the pace calculator.

Tips for pacing your 5K

  • Warm up properly. Jog easily for 10–15 minutes and add a few short strides. A 5K starts fast, and there is little time to settle in once the race begins.
  • Control the first mile. Aim to run the first mile within a few seconds of goal pace. Going out too fast is the most common 5K mistake.
  • Push from the 2-mile mark. The last mile is where the race is decided. Save enough to hold pace, then speed up over the final half mile.
  • Learn the pace on the track. The 400 m lap column shows your goal pace for one lap of a standard track, which makes it easy to practice with repeats.
