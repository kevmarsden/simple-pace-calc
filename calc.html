How to use the pace calculator

Fill in any two fields and press the button for the one you want. The calculator also shows your speed and a split for every mile or kilometer.

  • Pace needs a time and a distance. Example: 1:45:00 for 13.1 miles is 8:01 per mile.
  • Time needs a distance and a pace. Example: 26.2 miles at 9:00 per mile is 3:55:48.
  • Distance needs a time and a pace. Example: 45 minutes at 7:30 per mile is 6 miles.

Entering times

  • A number with no colons is minutes: 23 is 23 minutes.
  • One colon is minutes and seconds: 23:30 is 23 minutes, 30 seconds.
  • Two colons is hours, minutes, and seconds: 1:23:30 is 1 hour, 23 minutes, 30 seconds.

Distances and units

Enter a distance in miles or kilometers, or pick a race distance from the list. The pace unit controls the splits: choose /mi for mile splits or /km for kilometer splits. See the guide to calculating running pace for the formulas behind the calculator.
